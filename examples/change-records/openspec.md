# Faster export

## Why
Customers wait for large exports.

## What changes
Queue exports and notify customers when complete.

## Expected outcome
More customers complete their exports.

## Hypothesis
Background processing will reduce abandoned exports.

## Success signals
- Export completion rate

## Baseline
72% completed in the previous month.

## Target
85% completion.

## Review window
30 days after release.

## Guardrails
- Exported rows must remain complete.
