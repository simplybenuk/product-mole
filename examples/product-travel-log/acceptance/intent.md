# Search handles typing mistakes

## Change ID
change-search-typos

## Problem
New accounts abandon searches after a typing mistake.

## Expected outcome
More new accounts complete a search.

## Success signals
- Search completion percentage among eligible searches by new accounts

## Baseline
50 percent in the week before release

## Target
65 percent in the week after release

## Review window
2026-09-08 through 2026-09-15, end exclusive
