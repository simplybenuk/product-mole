# Intent: CSV export for reports

## Problem

Finance and operations users copy report tables into spreadsheets by hand.
Support logged 31 tickets asking for export between May and July 2026.

## Intended outcome

- Fewer support tickets asking for export.
- Weekly active report viewers on the Team plan keep using reports instead of
  rebuilding them elsewhere.

## Expected measures

| Measure | Baseline | Expected after 4 weeks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export-request tickets per week | 3 to 4 | 1 or fewer |
| Reports with at least one export per week | 0 | 15% of active reports |

## Not expected

No change to new sign-ups or plan upgrades is expected from this change.
